FOLLOWING ME? · TRACKER WATCHPROTO

Personal-safety check for item trackers (AirTag-style Find My, Tile, Chipolo, SmartTag, Google Find Hub, DULT tags) that keep showing up near you. Mark your own tags as mine so they never alert. If an unknown tracker alerts: use FIND to locate it, check bags, coat and car, and use your phone's built-in unknown-tracker alerts. If you feel unsafe, contact local law enforcement.

Rule: tracker-type device, not marked mine, nearby ≥ the time above, with mid/strong signal (≥ −80 dBm) at least 60% of that time. Trackers rotate their Bluetooth address (often every ~15 min near the owner), so one tag can appear as a new ID — NEXUS groups by brand + type to soften that, but this is an estimate, not proof.

How to sweep: hold the phone flat at chest height and turn slowly in a full circle (~20 s). Your body blocks signal behind you, so the heading where it reads strongest is the best guess. HOW TO GO LIVE

iPhone / iPad: Safari can't do Bluetooth — and neither can Chrome, Edge or Firefox on iPhone (Apple makes them all use Safari's engine). Install the free Bluefy – Web BLE Browser app, open this page in it, and allow Bluetooth. Then tap GO LIVE · PICK DEVICE or + TRACK A DEVICE (the button label matches what this browser can do). Bluefy lets you pick devices one at a time; if a device can't give a signal reading, NEXUS measures how easily it connects instead — shown as link %, not dBm. A full nearby scan and passive Tracker Watch on iPhone need the planned native NEXUS iOS app later — this web cut stays honest about that limit.

Android: Use Chrome. Turn on Bluetooth and Location, and allow Chrome “Nearby devices”. To see everything around you, open chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features, set it to Enabled, relaunch. Without the flag you can still track devices one at a time.

Computer: Chrome or Edge on Windows/Mac (same flag for the full scan). Firefox and Safari can't do Bluetooth.

Live Mode only reads the public “I'm here” broadcasts devices already send. It doesn't pair, doesn't connect (except GATT Explorer and Bluefy's link check, which only touch the one device you picked), and nothing leaves your phone. Many phones change their Bluetooth ID every few minutes on purpose, so the same phone can show up as a “new” device.

NEXUS is a personal-safety and find-my-stuff tool: locate your own keys, earbuds and tags, and check whether an unknown item tracker is travelling with you. Distances and directions are estimates, not proof.
